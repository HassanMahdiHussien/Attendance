What you need: a report you submitted, or an email you forwarded into the intake mailbox.

![The My reports list](shots/my-reports)

1. Open **My reports** from the navigation. Your own reports appear with a number and status.
2. Read **Received** when HR has not completed intake. **Under review** means HR is handling the item. A report can then show **Converted to case** or **Dismissed**.
3. If you forwarded an email, look for **Forwarded email** on the same page. Its visible status can be **Received**, **Under review**, or **Closed**.
4. Select **+ New report** if you need to submit another incident. Follow [submit a report](help:reporting/submit-report) for the fields and attachments.

Good to know:

- This list shows a report's status. It does not expose HR's duplicate check, the linked case, or another employee's case history.
- A forwarded email is a draft for HR intake. HR confirms the structured facts before opening a case.
- If you need details beyond the status, contact your HR team through your company's normal channel. The report page has no case approval or document issuance controls.
