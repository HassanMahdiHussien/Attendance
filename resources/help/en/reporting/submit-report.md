What you need: a reporter or department manager account assigned to a branch. HR and company administrators receive reports in **Reports inbox** and open cases through their own workflow.

![The report form](shots/submit-report)

1. Open **My reports**, then **+ New report**. Choose **Branch** and, if known, **Employee concerned**. Select **Additional employees in this incident** when the same incident concerns several employees.
2. Choose **Location**, or write **Place if not listed**. Enter **Incident date and time** and **Violation type**. If the type is absent, choose **Other — describe below** and use **Violation if not listed**.
3. Describe only what you know in **Incident description**. Add **Alleged responsible person** from the employee list or **Name if not listed**. Enter **Witnesses, separated by commas** if applicable.
4. Add files under **Attachments (PDF or images, up to 10 MB per file)** and select **Send report**. You return to **My reports** after submission.

Good to know:

- If the page says your account has no branch, ask HR to assign one before submitting.
- The incident time cannot be in the future. A place or listed location and a description are required.
- HR reviews the report before opening a case. You can follow its status in [track your reports](help:reporting/track-your-reports).
