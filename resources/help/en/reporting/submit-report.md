---

What you need: a reporter or department manager account assigned to a branch. HR and company administrators receive reports in **Reports inbox** and open cases through their own workflow.

![The report assistant](shots/submit-report)

1. Open **My reports**, then **+ New report**. Under **What happened?**, write what you saw in your own words, as you would tell a colleague: who was involved, where and when, if you know. Select **Start report**.
2. The assistant asks short questions only about what is missing, usually one or two. Type your answer and select **Send**, or tap a quick reply such as **I do not know**. The bar at the top shows which items are already answered.
3. When everything needed is in, select **Review report**. Read the summary at the top: it was written by the assistant from your own words, so check it. Below it are the people, place and time, and what happened.
4. To add photos or files, select **Add photos or files**. If something is wrong, select **Edit report**. When it is right, select **Confirm and send**. You return to **My reports**.

Good to know:

- The assistant never adds facts you did not give. A date you wrote is kept as you wrote it, and a time you did not give is left empty.
- If you prefer, select **Fill in the form instead** to use the plain form. If the assistant cannot finish, the form opens with your answers kept.
- A report cannot be changed after sending. HR reviews it before opening a case. You can follow its status in [track your reports](help:reporting/track-your-reports).
