---
title: Upload reports
summary: Upload employee, leave balance, leave and attendance reports together.
audience: hr_staff
order: 125
---

Open **Upload** at the start of the navigation or use **Upload files** on the HR dashboard. Choose CSV or XLSX files for the employee list, annual leave balance, leave report or attendance transactions. Employee lists also accept TXT. You may select several files together.

For a recognised layout, check the short summary and choose **Import**. Attendance coverage dates and branches are pre-filled; choose **Change** if the file covers something different. The declaration determines which days may be assessed, so check it before importing a partial export.

If a layout is new, match its columns and update the preview. A balance report needs its balance date and year. An employee list defaults to a partial update; choose **Change** for a full list or another effective date. Unmatched employee branches can be linked to an existing branch or created with a jurisdiction. If more than half the attendance codes are unknown, confirm that it is the right file. A file already imported cannot be imported again.

The page imports ready files in this order: employees, leave balance, leave report, attendance. Rows needing a decision stay on the page. After import, use each row's batch link to follow progress. **Advanced upload** opens the older individual upload pages.
