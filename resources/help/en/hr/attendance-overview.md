The **Attendance dashboard** opens on the current month. Choose an employee, branch, department, manager, work pattern or status, then apply the filters. Each tile opens the employees behind that result. Open an employee to review the daily record and the source links.

The employee attendance section has quick periods for the current month, previous month and last 30 days, plus a custom date range. Its daily record shows current assessed days only. **Pending** means the day is not yet complete or covered; it is not an absence. Open **Day detail** for the calculation and leave explanation, or **Raw and cleaned punches** to compare device records with their cleaned interpretation.

The leave, credit, changes and issues tabs show the records behind the employee's result. Source imports and calculation runs have separate timestamps in **Last data update**. **Results are updating** means a newer source has not yet been fully processed. Coverage quality counts working days that can be assessed for each branch.
