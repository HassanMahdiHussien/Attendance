---
title: Record a committee session
summary: Enter attendees, votes, minutes, and the human decision.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 70
---

What you need: an issued defense letter and either a response or the end of the 3-day response period. Open the case and select **Record committee session**.

1. Select the attendees from the eligible committee pool. Choose at least three distinct people, including a **Labor union** representative. Select a **Session chair** and **Minutes author** from those attending.
2. Enter **Session minutes** and each attendee's **Vote**. Choose **Outcome** and **Employee defense considered** from the recorded human decision, then select **Save session**.
3. For a shared incident, enter **Shared session minutes (internal)**. For each ready employee case, write **Minutes for this employee**, its **Outcome**, **Employee defense considered**, and each attendee's vote. The employee-specific minutes enter that case's recommendations draft.
4. If the session does not reach a majority of attendees voting **Approve**, record another vote before writing recommendations. Once a majority is recorded, continue with [recommendations](help:hr/recommendations).

Good to know:

- A conflicted pool member cannot be selected. HR must resolve a name-only match with a reason; a direct employee-linked conflict needs a substitute.
- Do not put another incident member's name in the minutes for this employee.
- The session records a human outcome: **Recommendation without penalty**, **Warning**, or **Termination**. AI only drafts wording from that decision.
