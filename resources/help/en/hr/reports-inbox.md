---
title: Review the reports inbox
summary: Turn a report or forwarded email into case intake, link it, or dismiss it.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 10
---

What you need: HR or company administrator access. Open **Reports inbox** from the navigation.

![The reports inbox](shots/reports-inbox)

1. Read the report's description, people, attachments, and any open intake questions. Forwarded messages appear as **Forwarded email**. Check the original facts before you act.
2. If a report needs a listed type, choose **Map to a violation type** and select **Map violation type**. For a new case, select **Convert to case**. Complete the case draft and intake questions before opening it.
3. For a report naming one employee, inspect **Link to a duplicate case**. It offers eligible existing cases near the reported incident time. Choose a case and select **Convert to case** to attach the report instead of making a new case.
4. For a forwarded email, select **Create case** to review its facts in the new-case form, or choose **Link to an existing case** and **Link to case**.
5. If the item should not proceed, enter **Dismissal reason** and select **Dismiss**.

Good to know:

- Duplicate candidates use the incident time within 48 hours. Reporters do not see this check.
- An email is not converted automatically. HR completes and confirms structured facts in the case draft.
- Several employees in one report can become linked cases in an incident. See [open a case](help:hr/open-a-case).
