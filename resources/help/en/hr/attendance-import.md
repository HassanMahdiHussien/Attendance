---
title: Import attendance and review punches
summary: Upload transactions, confirm coverage, approve devices, and review issues.
audience: hr_staff
order: 140
---

Open **Attendance → Upload attendance**. Upload a CSV or XLSX export. For a new header layout, check each column mapping. The mapping is saved for future files with the same headers.

Confirm the suggested date range and branches. A branch without an approved device is marked **not confirmable yet**. The file and declaration can be imported, but that branch's coverage remains unconfirmed until an appropriate device is approved.

Review the preview's new and already stored transactions, duplicates, devices, unknown IDs and row errors. Confirm the import, then follow its progress. The **Attendance batches** page shows earlier uploads, counts, failures and a retry option. You can download the original private file there.

Open **Devices** to approve a pending serial number, choose its role and branch, and set an effective date. Later changes and retirement reprocess affected punches. Open **Data issues** to resolve or dismiss an issue with a note.

The **Punch viewer** shows original transactions beside their cleaned status and meal count for one person and date range. It does not calculate attendance or absence.
