title: View an employee's case history

---

What you need: access to employee records in your company. Open **Employees** from the navigation.

![An employee page](shots/employee-page)

1. Use **Search employees** with a name, employee number, or department. Select an employee in the results to open the employee page.
2. Check **Hire date** and the summary counters: **Total cases**, **Warnings**, and **Last 12 months**.
3. Under **Disciplinary history**, review each visible case's date, violation type, location, stage or outcome, and description. Select the **Case** link for the full case record and document history.
4. If an employee is named in an incident with others, the entry carries an **Incident** badge. Open the individual case to review only the facts and actions available to you.

Good to know:

- The page can say **There may be other cases you cannot see**. Its counters and history follow your access scope; do not treat a partial view as a complete record.
- When HR opens a new case, the case page can show **Prior record**, same-type repeats within 12 months, and a separate repeated alleged responsible-person pattern.
- A prior case is context for review. It does not automatically decide the new case's outcome. See [open a case](help:hr/open-a-case).
