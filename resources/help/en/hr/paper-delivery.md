---
title: Record paper delivery
summary: Log a signature, refusal to sign, or refused delivery.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 50
---

What you need: an issued defense letter, an employee response handoff, or an issued decision letter. The physical paper is the official delivery.

1. On the case page, find **Record paper handoff**. Before a response is recorded, choose **Defense letter receipt** or **Response submission**. After a decision letter is issued, use **Paper decision letter receipt**.
2. If the person signs, choose **Signed**, enter **Signing date**, and select **Record handoff**.
3. If the paper was received but the person would not sign, choose **Received and refused to sign**. Enter **First witness** and **Second witness** and upload **Paper signed by both witnesses (PDF, JPG or PNG)** before selecting **Record handoff**.
4. If the person would not accept the paper, choose **Refused delivery**. Add the two witnesses and signed witness paper, plus **Attempt date**, **Attempt location**, and **Person making the attempt**. Then select **Record handoff**.

Good to know:

- **Received and refused to sign** and **Refused delivery** record different events. Choose the one that matches the paper handoff.
- The same paper rule applies at defense letter receipt, response submission, and decision letter receipt.
- Email is an alert, not legal delivery. Read [paper and signatures](help:rules/paper-and-signatures).
