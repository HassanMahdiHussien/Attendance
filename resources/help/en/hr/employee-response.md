---
title: Record the employee response
summary: Upload the paper response and confirm any transcription against the scan.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 60
---

What you need: an issued defense letter and the employee's paper response, if one was submitted.

1. Record **Response submission** under **Record paper handoff** using the correct signature or refusal status. Follow [paper delivery](help:hr/paper-delivery) for the witness requirements.
2. Under **Employee response scan**, choose the PDF or image of the paper response and select **Upload scan**.
3. The scan remains the original. You can type **Transcribed text** yourself. To request an AI reading, first confirm **I checked that the image contains no identity number, salary, or phone number**, then select **Read response with AI**.
4. Compare the proposed text with the displayed scan. Correct every reading error, choose **Source**, and select **Confirm text after checking against the image**. The committee sees the confirmed text as context.

Good to know:

- AI reading is only a draft transcription. It does not replace the scan or make a decision. If it fails, enter the text manually.
- After the 3-day response deadline has passed, the case page offers **Record no response** if no response was logged. You may then proceed to the committee session.
- If you later find a text mistake, use **Reason for text correction** and **Correct text** while that action is available.
