What you need: a role with **Statistics** access. Open **Statistical reports** from that navigation item.

![Statistical reports](shots/statistics)

1. Choose a tab under **Report type**. Set **Period** to **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Custom**. Enter **Year**, **Month**, **Quarter**, or **From** and **To** as the chosen period requires.
2. If offered, choose **Company** and **Branch**. For an employee report, choose **Employee**. Select **View report** to preview the result.
3. Check the preview's period and scope. If it says **There may be other cases you cannot see**, the visible rows are partial. If it says **The first 1,000 rows are shown — use Excel for the full list**, use the Excel export for the complete list.
4. Select **Export PDF** or **Export Excel**. The request appears in **My exports**; select **Download** when ready.

Good to know:

- Company HR and administrators see their company. HR partners see linked companies. Platform administrators can choose a company and, for some report types, **All companies**.
- Employee-level reports are offered only where your role and company access allow them. Changing the company resets branch and employee choices.
- Exports capture the selected scope and language when requested.
