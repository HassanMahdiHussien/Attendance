---
title: Record an appeal and archive
summary: Log a filed appeal and its outcome, or close a recommendation-only case.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 110
---

What you need: signed recommendations for a recommendation-only case, or an issued decision letter for a penalty case.

1. For **Recommendation without penalty**, confirm the signed recommendations scan is recorded. If consultation was requested, wait until it is completed or cancelled. Select **Archive without a penalty** on the case page. For a shared incident, enter **Reason to close this employee’s case** first.
2. For a penalty, open **Record appeal and outcome** on the case page. Check **Appeal window**. If an appeal was filed within it, enter **Appeal filing date** and select **Record appeal**.
3. Once the appeal has an outcome, enter **Appeal outcome** and select **Record outcome and archive**. The case moves to the archive with the recorded result.

Good to know:

- The 30-day appeal window starts on the date the final decision letter is issued. HR records an appeal manually; the system does not detect filings at an external court.
- A filed appeal needs its outcome before archive. If no appeal is recorded by the end of the window, the system archives the case automatically.
- All days are calendar days. Nothing pauses the clock. See [deadlines](help:rules/deadlines).
