What you need: HR or company administrator access with the **Dashboard** item visible in your navigation.

![The HR dashboard](shots/dashboard)

1. Open **Dashboard**. Select **Branch** and **Period** to set the view. For **Custom period**, enter **From** and **To**.
2. Read **Deadline alerts** first. They can identify an approaching or overdue procedure or response deadline, a pending legal consultation, or an appeal window ending soon. Open the linked case to act on its next step.
3. Use **Metrics** to review **Open cases**, **Closed this month**, outcomes, the **15-day deadline** measures, and **Average procedure days** for the selected scope.
4. Read **Repeat alerts** and **Repeat hotspots** to identify recurring areas such as branches, locations, violation types, and alleged responsible people. Open the case record before taking any case action.

Good to know:

- New reports appear first in **Needs action** and have their own counter linking to the inbox.

- Dashboard counts and patterns reflect the selected branch and period and the cases you can see.
- An alert is a prompt to review the case; the case page shows the actual due events and recorded action. See [case board](help:hr/case-board) and [deadlines](help:rules/deadlines).
- **Statistical reports** provides a separate preview and export workflow.
