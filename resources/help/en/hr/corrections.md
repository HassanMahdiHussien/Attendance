---
title: Correct an issued document
summary: Start a new version with a reason instead of editing the issued record.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 120
---

What you need: an issued document on a case you may manage and a clear correction reason.

1. Open the case page and find **Documents and versions**. Identify the issued **Defense request letter**, **Committee recommendations**, or **Decision letter** that needs correction.
2. Enter **Correction reason** beside that locked document and select **Start correction**. The system makes a new draft version and keeps the original issued version visible in the history.
3. Edit the new draft in the document's editor. Review all enabled languages, citations, names, and dates. Follow the same submission and confirmation path required for that document type.
4. Before issuing the correction, check **Document preview** and **Reference number**. Select **Confirm and issue** only after the new version is ready to be printed and handled on paper.

Good to know:

- The issued version is immutable; a correction never silently changes its PDF or text.
- A corrected document does not restart the original response, procedure, or appeal deadline anchors.
- Editing recommendations resets prior approvals on the text being revised. Read [recommendations](help:hr/recommendations) before resubmitting them.
