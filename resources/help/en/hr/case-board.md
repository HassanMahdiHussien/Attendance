What you need: HR or company administrator access. Open **Cases** to reach **Case board**.

![The case board](shots/case-board)

1. Use **Search** to find an employee name or case number. Narrow the list with **Branch** and **Stage**. Select an active filter to remove it, or clear all filters.
2. Read the five dots under **Progress**. They mark the case's current stage. Open the case number or employee name to see its actions, due dates, documents, and event timeline.
3. Check **Status** before choosing a case. The board can show **Awaiting response**, **Awaiting signature**, **Overdue**, or **Closed**. A banner at the top warns when a case approaches the 15-day legal deadline.
4. Select **Open new case** to start intake. Use [open a case](help:hr/open-a-case) for the required review before opening.

Good to know:

- The board is limited to cases you may see. Branch and stage choices are remembered as your view preferences.
- The case page has separate response, procedure, and appeal due events. See [deadlines](help:rules/deadlines) for their start dates.
- For company-wide counters, alerts, and patterns, open [Dashboard](help:hr/dashboard).
