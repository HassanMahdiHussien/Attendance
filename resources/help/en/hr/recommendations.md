title: Issue committee recommendations
summary: Draft Doc A, collect three approvals, and record the signed scan.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff, committee_member
order: 80
---

What you need: a committee session with a majority decision. HR manages the draft; eligible committee members review it.

1. HR selects **Write recommendations** on the case. Use **Draft with AI** when available or enter **Findings** and **Recommendations** for every enabled language. Check the citations and drafting notes. Select **Save**, then **Submit for approvals**.
2. An eligible pool member opens **Committee**, then **Review committee recommendations**. Read every language. Choose **Approval category** and select **Approve**, or enter **Rejection comment** and select **Reject with comment**.
3. HR addresses a rejection, edits the text, and resubmits. Editing resets all earlier approvals. The document needs one separate in-system approval from **Legal**, **HR**, and **Labor union**.
4. HR opens **Review recommendation approvals**, checks the document, selects **Confirm and issue**, prints it, and collects paper signatures from the actual attendees.
5. Open **Recommendations and signatures scan**. Upload **Signed recommendations scan** and record each attendee's box as filled or empty after reviewing the paper. You may choose **Upload and read with AI** for a suggestion, then confirm the states yourself.

Good to know:

- An empty box blocks progress unless a company administrator records **Reason for overriding empty box** and **Record override**. AI checks presence, never signer identity.
- A recommendation without a penalty can close after the signed scan and any pending consultation. A penalty proceeds to [the decision letter](help:hr/decision-letter).
