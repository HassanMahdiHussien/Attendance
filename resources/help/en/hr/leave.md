---
title: Import and reconcile leave
summary: Upload leave reports, review requests, map types, and explain attendance credits.
audience: hr_staff
order: 160
---

Open **Leave → Upload leave** and choose a CSV or Excel report. On a new header layout, check the column mapping. Choose whether the report is partial or full, verify its snapshot time, and set the full report period when needed. The preview shows new, changed and unchanged requests, invalid dates, unknown employees and unmapped types. Review the bounded error samples before importing. The progress panel shows the batch's processing events and remains available after reloading the page. **Leave batches** lists previous imports and lets you download an audited copy of the original file.

Use **Leave requests** to filter by status, type, employee, dates or a flagged issue. Open a form number to compare source versions, distributed days, allocations, credits and HR decisions. A rejected date takes precedence over the report's approval stage. A full report can flag a previously seen request as missing; it does not cancel that request. For a flagged distribution, confirm the working dates or correct its dates and duration with a reason. Cancellation and withdrawal are recorded as new decisions.

**Leave types** lists unmapped types first. Review whether a type covers attendance and what entitlement it consumes, then save the mapping with a reason. **Credit ledger** shows each employee's credit source, use, available amount and expiry. Credits are applied automatically in date order to uncovered balances. You may reverse a specific deduction with a reason; it stays unreconciled until the reversal decision is withdrawn. The credit expiry setting is company wide.

Open an **Attendance day** to see the leave amounts reconciled against absence, lateness, early departure and exits. Its remaining amounts and explanations come from the current allocation ledger. Earlier attendance day results remain unchanged.
