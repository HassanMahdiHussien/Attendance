---
title: Review office attendance
summary: Read daily results, correct missing times, and manage the office policy.
audience: hr_staff
order: 150
---

Open **Attendance → Daily attendance** to choose a date, branch and status. The counts summarize the filtered branch and date. Each employee row shows first in, last out, elapsed time, recorded presence, lateness, early departure, short hours and the balance requiring reconciliation. Times and durations use hours and minutes. Open a row to see its source intervals and calculation history.

An ongoing or incompletely covered day stays **Pending**. A covered day with one unmatched punch becomes **Missing punch**. A covered working day with no punch becomes **Absent**, unless a suspected check-in device outage is recorded for the branch. These results remain open to review; absence is not an automatic HR decision.

Open **Attendance → Review queue** for missing punches and unreviewed absences. Choose **Review day** to add a check-in or check-out time with a reason, or to confirm an absence. Manual times remain separate from device punches. Use the **Reviewed days** filter to withdraw a correction or confirmation with a reason. The day is recalculated after each action, and earlier results remain in its detail history.

Open **Attendance → Office policy** to see the current rule values and previous versions. Add a version with its effective date and a reason. A back-dated version recalculates affected days; the day detail shows the policy version and employee schedule used for each result.
