---
title: Request legal consultation
summary: Send an open case for external review and follow its status.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff, committee_member
order: 90
---

What you need: access to the open case and a reason to seek an external legal opinion.

1. On the case page, select **Request legal consultation**. Read the confirmation warning: the 15-day procedure deadline keeps running while you wait. Confirm the request.
2. Watch the case page for the pending consultation notice. The request is recorded immediately, but the partner law firm can open the case only after the signed committee recommendations are uploaded.
3. After the reviewer completes the consultation, read the note on the case page. HR then continues the applicable outcome path: close a recommendation-only case, or review and issue a decision letter for a penalty.

Good to know:

- Consultation is optional and request-only. Even a termination case is not sent automatically.
- A request can be made before the signed recommendations; the external reviewer sees the case after that document is signed and scanned.
- For a recommendation-only case after signed recommendations, a company administrator can enter **Reason to cancel consultation** and select **Cancel consultation** while the request remains pending.
- The consultation never pauses the [procedure deadline](help:rules/deadlines). A pending consultation appears in the case and dashboard alerts.
