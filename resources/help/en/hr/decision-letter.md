---
title: Issue a decision letter
summary: Confirm a penalty decision and issue the paper letter.
audience: company_admin, hr_staff
order: 100
---

What you need: issued, signed committee recommendations with a **Warning** or **Termination** outcome, and any requested consultation completed or cancelled.

1. Check the decision draft on the case. The system may request an AI draft after the signed recommendations scan; a required regulation comparison or AI failure may leave you with the manual path. Select **Write decision letter** and write the reasons in every enabled language when needed.
2. Confirm that the letter reflects **Employee defense considered** as **Fully**, **Partially**, or **Not accepted** from the session record. The outcome and penalty are printed from the case record; do not change them in prose.
3. For termination, confirm **The employee is not on statutory leave or documented sick absence** and select **Record confirmation**. The system records who confirmed it and when.
4. Select **Save draft**, then **Submit for review**. Open **Confirm decision letter**, check names, dates, citations, and **Reference number**, then select **Confirm and issue**.
5. Print the issued letter for the **General Manager** and **Union representative** to sign on paper. On the case page, record their optional signature box states with **Record box states**, and record the employee's [paper receipt](help:hr/paper-delivery).

Good to know:

- The decision letter has no separate committee approval gate. It follows approved recommendations and human confirmation.
- Issuance starts the 30-day [appeal window](help:hr/appeal-and-archive). A recommendation-only case has no decision letter or appeal window.
