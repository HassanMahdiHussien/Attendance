1. If you cannot see a page mentioned in a guide, check [finding your way](help:getting-started/finding-your-way). Navigation follows your role. A reporter follows **My reports**; HR handles cases and the inbox.
2. If a submitted report has not become a case, check its visible status in [track your reports](help:reporting/track-your-reports). HR reviews structured facts and possible duplicates before opening a case.
3. If a letter is waiting, read [deadlines](help:rules/deadlines) and the case's due-event table. The 3-day response and 15-day procedure periods begin when the defense letter is issued. A pending consultation does not stop them.
4. If AI cannot prepare wording or read a scan, use [how AI helps](help:rules/how-ai-helps). Type the text yourself and check the original paper.
5. If an issued document has an error, HR uses [corrections](help:hr/corrections) to make a new version with a reason. The issued copy remains locked.

Good to know:

- Official delivery is on paper. Read [paper and signatures](help:rules/paper-and-signatures) before recording a refused handoff or a signature box.
- A recommendation-only case closes after HR uploads the signed recommendations and any requested consultation is completed or cancelled. Only a penalty decision starts an appeal window.
