title: Words used in the case workflow
summary: Match common guide terms to records and stages in Displanery.
audience: all
order: 20
---

1. A **Report** is information sent for HR review. It can remain **Received**, move **Under review**, be **Converted to case**, or be **Dismissed**. Read [submit a report](help:reporting/submit-report).
2. A **Case** is the individual employee's disciplinary record. An **Incident** can group separate cases for several employees involved in one event. The **Case board** shows progress and status.
3. The **Defense request letter** asks for the employee's response. The **Committee session** records attendees, minutes, votes, and outcome. **Committee recommendations** are the document approved by legal, HR, and labor-union pool members.
4. A **Decision letter** is issued only for a penalty outcome. **Legal consultation** is an optional external review requested by an eligible user, after which HR continues the case.
5. **Appeal** is the manually recorded challenge within the 30-day window after a decision letter. **Closed** means the case has reached its archive state.

Good to know:

- The actual screen labels depend on your chosen interface language. Use the linked guides for exact controls.
- **Signed** and **Refused delivery** describe different paper handoffs. A signature-box check records visible presence only. See [paper and signatures](help:rules/paper-and-signatures).
