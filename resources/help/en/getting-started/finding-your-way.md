---
title: Find your way around
summary: Use the navigation and phone menu to reach your workspace.
audience: all
order: 30
---

1. Start at **Home**. The attendance dashboard will be added in spec 505.
2. Use **Employees** to open the employee list and an employee page.
3. On a phone, open **More** for the remaining destinations, language choice, **Help**, and **Log out**.
4. The account menu offers the same language and sign-out choices on larger screens.

Navigation items appear according to your role and company access. See [employees](help:hr/employees-and-history) for the employee list and page.
