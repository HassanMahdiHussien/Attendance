title: Find your way around
summary: Use My work, navigation, and the phone menu to reach your tasks.
audience: all
order: 30
---

![The My work page](shots/home)

1. Start at **My work** if it appears in your navigation. It shows the work available to your role, including items that need action. Select the button on a task to open its record.
2. Use the main navigation for a specific area. HR can open **Cases** and **Reports inbox**. Reporters and department managers use **My reports**. Eligible committee members may see **Committee** for recommendations awaiting approval.
3. If you use a phone, the bottom navigation holds the first few items. Select **More** for the remaining destinations, settings, **Help**, language choice, and **Log out**.
4. Open **Help** and search for the task you are doing. The guide list is filtered for your role unless you choose to view all guides.

Good to know:

- A navigation item appears only when your role has access to that area. **Committee** can appear when an approval is pending.
- HR can use [the case board](help:hr/case-board) to find a case. A reporter can use [My reports](help:reporting/track-your-reports) to follow a submitted report.
- **My work** highlights a next task; it does not replace the case record or its document history.
