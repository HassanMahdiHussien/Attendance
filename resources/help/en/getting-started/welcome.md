Displanery helps your company manage disciplinary cases, from a report through review and closure. What you see depends on your role.

1. Open **My work**, if it appears in your navigation, to see the work available to you.
2. Use the navigation to open your reports, cases, or approvals.
3. Open **Help** whenever you need a guide.

Official letters are issued and delivered on paper. The platform records the work and keeps the case history.
