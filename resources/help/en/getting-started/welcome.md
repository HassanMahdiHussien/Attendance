Displanery records disciplinary work from a report to a closed case. Your account shows the work allowed for your role.

1. Open **My work**, if it appears in your navigation, to see tasks or records assigned to your role. Reporters see **My reports**; HR sees case actions and incoming reports; committee members see approvals when they are eligible.
2. If your role allows it, open **Cases** to follow an individual case. The five progress dots mark case opening, defense letter, employee response, committee work, and decision. The case status also shows when it is closed.
3. Use **Help** in the account menu for the step you are taking. On a phone, open **More** to reach the rest of the menu and **Help**.

Good to know:

- A report does not itself issue a letter. HR reviews the facts and opens a case.
- The employee receives official letters on paper. Email only alerts an internal user to work in the platform.
- The committee records its session and approves recommendations. HR handles document issuance and paper delivery.
