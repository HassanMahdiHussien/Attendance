Your workspace provides access to employee records and company settings according to your role.

1. Open **Home** to see the dashboard placeholder.
2. Open **Employees** to find an employee by name, code, or department.
3. Use **Help** in the account menu to find a guide. On a phone, open **More** for the rest of the menu.

Continue with [finding your way](help:getting-started/finding-your-way).
