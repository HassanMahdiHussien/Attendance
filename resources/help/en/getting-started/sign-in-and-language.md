---
title: Sign in and choose a language
summary: Enter your workspace and change the interface language.
audience: all
order: 20
---

What you need: an active account and the email address and password supplied for it.

1. Open the sign-in page. Enter **Email address** and **Password**, then select **Log in**.
2. After sign-in, use the account menu at the top of the workspace. Select **العربية** to change the interface to Arabic. When the interface is Arabic, select **English** to change it back.
3. On a phone, open **More** and use the same language choice near the bottom of that sheet.
4. Select **Log out** from the account menu, or from **More** on a phone, when you finish on a shared device.

Good to know:

- The interface starts in English and remembers your Arabic choice. Changing the interface language does not rewrite an issued document.
- Your account role determines which pages appear after sign-in. Use [finding your way](help:getting-started/finding-your-way) to identify your starting page.
- If sign-in fails, check the credentials with the person who manages your account. Do not share your password with another user.
