---
title: Sign in and choose a language
summary: Enter your workspace and change the interface language.
audience: all
order: 20
---

What you need: an active account and its email address and password.

1. Open the sign-in page. Enter **Email address** and **Password**, then select **Log in**.
2. Use the account menu to choose **العربية** or **English**.
3. On a phone, open **More** for the same language choice.
4. Select **Log out** when you finish on a shared device.

The interface remembers your language choice. Your account role determines which pages you can open. Use [finding your way](help:getting-started/finding-your-way) to identify your starting page.
