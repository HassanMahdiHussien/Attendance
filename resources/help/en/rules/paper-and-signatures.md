---
title: Understand paper and signatures
summary: Keep official delivery and signature checks tied to the paper record.
audience: all
order: 20
---

1. Treat the issued paper letter as the official document given to the employee. A system email only alerts an internal user that work is waiting; it is not delivery of a defense or decision letter.
2. For defense letter receipt, response submission, and decision letter receipt, record the paper handoff. A signed receipt needs its signing date. If the person received the paper but refused to sign, record two witnesses and the paper they signed.
3. If the person refused the paper itself, record a separate refused-delivery attempt with date, place, person who attempted delivery, two witnesses, and their signed paper. HR can follow [paper delivery](help:hr/paper-delivery) for the exact controls.
4. When HR uploads signed committee recommendations, record whether each attendee's signature box looks filled or empty. Review any AI suggestion against the scan yourself.

Good to know:

- A filled box is a presence observation, not proof of who signed. The system does not authenticate signatures or signer identity.
- An empty recommendations box blocks progress unless a company administrator records a reasoned override. The original scan remains available for review.
- Decision-letter paper signatures belong to the General Manager and union representative; their box states can be recorded on the case page after issuance.
