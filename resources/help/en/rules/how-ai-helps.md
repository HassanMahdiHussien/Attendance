---
title: How AI helps with drafting
summary: Use AI suggestions while keeping facts, decisions, and confirmation with people.
audience: all
order: 30
---

1. Enter and check the facts yourself before asking for **Draft with AI**. For a defense letter, the draft uses the case's incident facts. For recommendations, the committee's recorded minutes, votes, and outcome come first.
2. Read **Drafting notes** for missing or unclear points. Review the draft in every enabled language and check **Citation check** against the official references shown by the system. The AI may suggest wording and checks; it does not supply new facts or decide the result.
3. Use **Save draft** and the document preview to review what will be issued. A person must select **Confirm and issue** after checking the finished text, names, dates, and reference number.
4. If the AI service is unavailable, write the text manually in the editor. Continue the same review and confirmation steps. For response scans, check any proposed **Transcribed text** against the image before confirming it.

Good to know:

- A signature-box AI suggestion says only whether a box appears filled or empty. It never verifies signer identity.
- Arabic is authoritative in multilingual issued documents. Check the English rendering against it.
- AI failure does not pause any [deadline](help:rules/deadlines).
