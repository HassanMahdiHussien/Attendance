title: Read the case deadlines

audience: all
order: 10

What you need: a case with an issued letter to see an active due date.

![A case page with its due dates](shots/case-page)

1. Open a case you are allowed to see. In its due-event table, read **Response**, **Procedure**, and, when a decision letter exists, **Appeal**. Check **Due**, **Remaining**, and **Status** for each event.
2. Count the 3-day response period and the 15-day procedure period from the issue date of the **Defense request letter**. The case opening date and committee session date do not start those clocks.
3. For a penalty case, count the 30-day appeal window from issue of the **Decision letter**. HR records a filed appeal manually using [appeal and archive](help:hr/appeal-and-archive).
4. When the case shows **Approaching** or **Overdue**, open the relevant next step promptly. HR also sees alerts on the case board and dashboard.

Good to know:

- These are calendar days. Weekends, holidays, Eid, and employee leave count. Nothing pauses the clocks, including a pending legal consultation.
- The response due day itself is included; **Record no response** appears after that day passes if no response was recorded.
- A recommendation-only case has no decision letter and therefore no 30-day appeal window.
