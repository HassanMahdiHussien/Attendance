# Feature Specification: Chat-style report assistant (step 2 of 2)

**Branch**: `056-report-assistant` (stacked on `055-report-people-picker`) | **Created**: 2026-10-18 | **Status**: Approved

**Input**: Product owner tested step 1 and wants the report to be a short conversation: the reporter writes what happened, the AI asks simple questions about what is missing, then shows a summary the reporter approves. Mockup approved: https://claude.ai/artifact/E8kgFqtNa6S7NNJVP1u6tJ
Decisions (2026-10-18): required before sending = who (≥1 employee or typed name), what happened, where, when (approximate ok), witnesses (or "none"); "I don't know" is an accepted, recorded answer for any item; how-it-started / injuries asked but optional. Max 6 assistant questions. Summary language = the language the reporter writes in (Arabic → Arabic, English → English). Reporters only in this step (HR New case later). «الشخص المسؤول المزعوم» is detected by the AI from the conversation and confirmed by the reporter (CLAUDE.md §3 repeat-person warning keeps working).

## Requirements
- **FR-001 Chat** on New report (reporter / dept manager): fixed opening prompt (not AI); the reporter writes freely; after each reporter message the assistant replies with ONE short question about the most important missing item, in the reporter's language, plain and neutral; quick-reply chips where useful (today / yesterday / other date; "none"; "I don't know"); stops when all required items are known or after 6 questions; a progress row shows which items are known.
- **FR-002 People**: names mentioned are matched to the employee list **server-side** within the reporter's branch (reuse `EmployeePicker`); the assistant asks the reporter to confirm a match ("Do you mean Rana Yaser Jabbar · EMP-0002?") with Yes / someone else (opens the step-1 picker) / not in list (keep typed name). Same for witnesses and for any other person the reporter says caused or ordered the act (→ alleged responsible person, optional).
- **FR-003 Summary** (the step-1 review screen, extended): structured fields (employees, witnesses, branch, location or place, date-time, violation type picked from the company list, alleged responsible person if any, injuries/damage) plus a short neutral narrative written **only** from the reporter's words ("According to the reporter…" / «أفاد المُبلّغ بأن…»); every field editable with the step-1 pickers before "Confirm and send". The reporter must confirm; nothing is saved before.
- **FR-004 What HR gets**: the confirmed fields, the summary, and the full conversation verbatim (stored with the report, immutable after sending, visible in the Reports inbox; never shown to other reporters).
- **FR-005 AI rules** (CLAUDE.md §3/§3b/§20): never invents facts, never accuses, never decides the violation or a penalty (violation type is a suggestion from the company's list, confirmed by the reporter); output via structured JSON schema; people are sent as masked tokens (server maps tokens ↔ employees); minimal payload (no IDs, salaries, phones); every call logged in `ai_generation_logs`.
- **FR-006 Fallback**: if the AI is unavailable / no key / out of credit / errors after retries, the step-1 form appears with the same required fields enforced (who, what, where, when, witnesses-or-none) — reporting never stops. The required-field rule also applies to the plain form generally (today it lets an empty report through).
- **FR-007 Model and cost**: model from config `displanery.ai.report_assistant_model` (env `REPORT_ASSISTANT_MODEL`, default `claude-haiku-5-5`); use prompt caching for the fixed system prompt + violation-type list; keep each turn small.
- **FR-008** Translations, Departures design, RTL, phone-friendly, behaviour inventory; tests with `FakeClaudeClient`: question flow and stop rule (≤6), required-item gate, "I don't know" accepted, server-side name matching and confirmation, masked payload (no raw names/IDs), summary uses only reporter facts (validator rejects fields not present in the conversation), language follows reporter, transcript stored and immutable, fallback form with required fields when AI fails, no AI call in tests with a real key.
