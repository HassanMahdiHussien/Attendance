# Feature Specification: Leave import, coverage and reconciliation

**Branch**: `504-leave-import-and-matching` | **Created**: 2026-10-22 | **Status**: Clarified 2026-10-22

**Input**: ROADMAP §5 spec 504. Requirements: PRD §4, §7, §11 (11.1–11.3), §12 (descriptions — the sample export has none), §17 (pending approver), §22, §25 tests 2, 13, 14, 15, 23; ROADMAP §2 decisions (statuses, rejection rule, Leave Entry, cancellation, covered types, 1 day = 8 h, 0.5 = 4 h) and Q2–Q4. Builds on 501 (employees), 503 (office days and balances).

## Sample file (design sample, real names — never committed)

No.`, `MyForm: Owner Name`, `Form Approver`, `Owner Location`, `Owner Department`, `Leave Status`, `Leave Type`, `Leave Type (Other)`, `Leave Starting Date`, `Leave End Date`, `Leave Duration (Work Days)`, `Leave Duration (WorkDays, Approved)` (always empty), `MyForm: Created By`, `MyForm: Created Date`, `Direct Manager`, `Leave Form Rejected`. 21,249 rows, dates `M/D/YYYY` text, some implausible (1970). ## Clarifications (product owner, 2026-10-22, plus ROADMAP §2 and Q2–Q4)

- **Statuses**: `Approved` covers (subject to type mapping). `Leave Entry` = saved, never submitted: covers nothing, nobody reminded. `Manager Approval` / `HR Approval` / `Department Manager Approval` = pending: covers nothing yet; the current approver is `Form Approver` (a name). **Rejected** whenever `Leave Form Rejected` has a date, whatever the status column says. **Cancellation** never appears in the report: HR marks a request cancelled manually; a request missing from a later full report raises an HR alert and is never cancelled automatically.
- **Types** (Q3, Q4): per-company leave-type mapping with a `covers` flag and a `consumes` tag (`none` | `rest_day_entitlement` for Compensation Leave | `overtime_or_credit` for Overtime Equivelant Leave — the matching itself comes with the overtime / entitlement specs; in 504 these types simply cover their days). Annual, Sick, Work From Home, Business Travel, Administrative, Unpaid (`Other`), Paternity, Funeral, Bereavement, Marriage, Court cover. `0200` and any unseen type do **not** cover until HR maps them; an unmapped type raises a data issue.
- **Hours**: 1 work day = 8 h, 0.5 = 4 h. A half-day's position in the day is never assumed.
- **Import quality refinements (product owner, 2026-10-22)**: one unknown-employee issue per owner code holds the request count and at most 20 form numbers, and resolves when that employee appears. Full-report period suggestions use plausible start dates only; invalid date rows never widen the suggested period.
- **Counting methods (product owner, 2026-10-22)**: the real 21,249-row report counts Saturday as a leave-system work day for many requests; some types count calendar days. Accept these known counts automatically in the specified order while assigning minutes only to employee working dates.
- **Same-day matching** (Q1 answer): an approved covering request reconciles hours **only on its own days**: a full-day leave reconciles that day's absence or balance; a half-day leave reconciles up to 4 h of that day's absence or balance (Q3 answer: a 4 h leave on an 8 h absence leaves the day absent with 4 h still unreconciled, pending HR). Leave on a day with attendance and nothing to reconcile raises a flag for HR ("leave with attendance, nothing to reconcile") — no purpose is invented.
- **Surplus credit** (PRD §11.2, **overridden by the product owner for its use**): hours of an approved leave not needed on its own day become employee credit (e.g. 4 h covering 3 h → 1 h credit). Credit is then **deducted automatically** from the employee's other unreconciled hours — absence, early departure or lateness — oldest unreconciled hours first, oldest credit first, each deduction with a stored, human-readable explanation (source request, its date, hours used, target day and kind). Optional credit expiry is a company setting (default: never). The same hour can never be spent twice; every allocation links one source to one target.
- **Reversal** (PRD §11.3): cancelling (HR) or rejecting (report) a request reverses its allocations, including credit it created and any automatic deductions that used that credit; the affected hours become unreconciled again and their flags reopen. No credit may remain without a valid source.

## Requirements

- **FR-001 Upload** (Filament, HR + platform admin): `.xlsx` / `.csv`, streamed; positional mapping saved per header signature (reuse the 501/502 pattern); declared report scope: **full report for a period** (requests missing from a later full report for an overlapping period raise an alert) or **partial**; preview before commit; the same file (hash) cannot be committed twice; original file kept on the private disk with an audited download.
- **FR-002 Requests and versions**: `leave_requests` keyed by `(company_id, source, external_id)` where `external_id` = form number and `source` = `company_report` (a future native leave module is another source). Each upload that shows a request with any changed field appends a `leave_request_versions` row (status, rejected date, type, dates, duration, approver, raw values, batch); the request row keeps the current values. Previous states are preserved (PRD §25 test 2). Append-only versions and batches.
- **FR-003 Data quality**: implausible dates (before 2000, after next year, end before start) and durations that do not fit the date span are isolated as data issues and the request covers nothing until HR fixes or confirms it; unknown employee codes produce one grouped data issue per owner code; unmapped types → data issue.
- **FR-004 Day distribution**: validate ceil(duration) against the first matching count: employee working days (effective-dated rest days), company leave-calendar days (leave_settings.leave_calendar_rest_days, default Friday [5]), then all calendar days. Store the method on the current distribution. Place 480 minutes per whole day and 240 for a final half day on employee working dates only, in order, never beyond the dates available in the span. A source-counted employee rest day carries no minutes. If no count matches, flag duration_mismatch and distribute nothing until HR confirms.
- **FR-005 Reconciliation**: per employee, deterministic, re-runnable allocation into `leave_allocations` (source = request day or credit; target = an office day's absence / lateness / early-departure minutes; minutes; kind; explanation; run id; reversed_by). Order: same-day leave first; then credit FIFO to the oldest unreconciled targets. Each office day (spec 503) shows reconciled vs remaining minutes, and an approved full-day leave on an absent day closes the absence automatically with the reference, reason and time (PRD §11).
- **FR-006 Pending requests**: shown on the day ("pending approval by <approver>"); they reconcile nothing until approved. Reminders to the approver come with the notifications spec.
- **FR-007 HR actions**: map leave types; mark a request cancelled (with reason) → reversal; confirm or fix a flagged request; view credit balance and allocations; manually reverse a credit deduction (it then stays unreconciled and is not re-deducted automatically — recorded as an HR decision).
- **FR-008 Screens**: leave upload; leave requests (filters: status, type, employee, date, flags); request detail (versions, days, allocations); leave types mapping; employee credit ledger (credits, deductions with explanations, balance). English first, Arabic RTL.
- **FR-009 Recalculation**: re-run reconciliation for affected employees and dates after a leave upload, an HR leave action, a type-mapping change, or a change of the employee's office days (spec 503 assessment) — superseding previous allocations, keeping their history.
- **FR-010 Permissions, isolation, audit**: HR of the company + platform admin; every table tenant-scoped with composite FKs and isolation tests; append-only versions, batches, HR decisions; audit rows for every HR action and reconciliation run.
- **FR-011 Tests**: PRD §25 2 (status change under the same ID updates and preserves history), 13 (4 h settles 3 h, 1 h credit — then auto-deducted from a later lateness with an explanation), 14 (leave + attendance with nothing to settle → flag, no fictitious reconciliation), 15 (cancellation reverses allocations including credit deductions and reopens hours), 23 (approved leave closes the absence; pending shows the approver); rejection-date rule; Leave Entry covers nothing; unmapped type `0200` covers nothing and raises an issue; half-day on an absent day leaves 4 h; multi-day distribution skipping rest days; duration mismatch flagged; request missing from a later full report → alert, not cancelled; re-upload idempotent; implausible dates isolated; isolation.

## Out of scope

Overtime and rest-day entitlements (Compensation / Overtime Equivalent leave only cover their days here); description AI; notifications and reminders; shift/security employees' days (their leave is imported and stored; matching applies once their days exist); profile and dashboard (505).
