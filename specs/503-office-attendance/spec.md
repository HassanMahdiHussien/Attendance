# Feature Specification: Office attendance — daily sessions, lateness, early departure, short hours, missing punches

**Branch**: `503-office-attendance` | **Created**: 2026-10-22 | **Status**: Clarified 2026-10-22

**Input**: ROADMAP §5 spec 503. Requirements: PRD §6 (office row), §6.1, §9, §10, §17 (device outage), §19 ("do not present an ongoing day as complete"), §22, §25 tests 6, 10, 11, 12; ROADMAP §2 (rest days Q1, no punches + no leave = absent). Builds on 501 (dated employee record) and 502 (cleaned punches, coverage). **Office employees only**: shift and security inference is a later spec; leave coverage is spec 504 (until then an absence or shortfall is shown as "not yet reconciled", never as a confirmed breach).

## Clarifications (product owner, 2026-10-22)

- **Late arrival (Q8)**: arrival after 09:00 is late by the minutes after 09:00; the required leaving time is then **18:00** (the end of the flexible window). Leaving before 18:00 is early departure. Staying later never cancels lateness.
- **Early arrival**: arrival before 08:00 counts as 08:00; the earliest leaving time stays **17:00**. Early minutes are not credited.
- **Temporary exits** (check-out then check-in during the day) reduce total recorded presence and are listed, but are **not** added to the reconciliation balance (company setting `exits_count_toward_balance`, default off).
- **When a day is assessed**: only once confirmed coverage (spec 502) for the employee's site reaches the end of their working day **plus 2 hours** (setting). Before that the day is **pending**. Absence and missing punches are always "pending HR review", never final automatically.

## Requirements

- **FR-001 Office policy (versioned)**: `attendance_policies` per company, effective-dated and append-only: office start 08:00, flexible window 60 min, workday 9 h including the 1 h break (so 08:00–17:00 … 09:00–18:00), minimum recorded presence 8 h, day-close grace 2 h, `exits_count_toward_balance` false. An employee's `schedule_start` / `schedule_end` (spec 501, read with `EmployeeHistory::asOf(date)`) override the start and end for that employee; the flexible window applies from their start. Every computed day stores the policy version and the employee values it used.
- **FR-002 Which days are assessed**: for each office employee (`work_pattern = office` as of that date) and each date: not before `hire_date`, not after `last_working_day`, not on a rest day (employee `rest_days` as of that date, else the office default Friday + Saturday), and only while the site was covered (FR-009). Employees with no work pattern are listed as "pattern missing" and not assessed; non-employees are never assessed.
- **FR-003 Daily session** from the employee's **current used** punches (spec 502) on that date: first check-in, last check-out, the list of intervals (check-in → next check-out), temporary exits (check-out → next check-in inside the day) and two time fields (PRD §9): **elapsed** (first in → last out) and **total recorded presence** (sum of intervals). No break is deducted.
- **FR-004 Lateness** = minutes of the first check-in after 09:00 (after `start + window`). **Required leaving** = arrival + 9 h for arrivals between 08:00 and 09:00; 17:00 for arrivals before 08:00; 18:00 for arrivals after 09:00 (per-employee schedule shifts these values). **Early departure** = minutes the last check-out is before the required leaving time. **Short hours** alert when total recorded presence < 8 h, with the shortfall.
- **FR-005 Balance requiring reconciliation** = lateness + early departure (+ temporary exits only if the setting is on), never counting a minute twice. Short hours is an alert shown separately and is **not** added (its minutes overlap lateness/early departure). The balance is "unreconciled" until spec 504 allocates leave to it.
- **FR-006 Day status** (one per employee per assessed date): `pending` (not yet covered past the required end + grace, or that time has not been reached), `present` (both in and out), `missing_punch` (only check-in or only check-out — pending HR review, the available punch kept), `absent` (no used punch on a covered working day — pending HR review / leave in 504), `rest_day`, `not_employed`, `pattern_missing`, `device_outage` (FR-008). Statuses are not mutually exclusive with measurements: a present day can have lateness and short hours at the same time.
- **FR-007 HR review of missing punches and absences** (PRD §10): HR (only) either **adds the missing time** — stored as a separate manual punch (actor, reason, timestamp, never shown as a device punch), which re-computes the day — or **confirms absence**, keeping the original punch. Each decision is append-only with before/after values and is audited. A correction can be reversed by a later decision.
- **FR-008 Device outage** (PRD §17): when a covered branch has **no** punches at all from its approved check-in devices during a working day on which it has office employees expected, raise one grouped `device_outage` data issue for that branch and date, and mark those employees' days `device_outage` (not absent) until HR resolves the issue (resolve = re-assess as normal; dismiss = treat as covered).
- **FR-009 Coverage**: a date is assessable for an employee only when a confirmed coverage interval of their branch (as of that date) covers the whole working day plus grace. Uncovered dates stay `pending`.
- **FR-010 Recalculation** (PRD §7): days are derived results (superseded per run like spec 502's punches; earlier results kept). Recompute only affected employee-days after: a punch processing run, an employee version change (from its effective date), a policy change (from its effective date), an HR decision, a device-outage resolution, a coverage change. Each run records what changed (previous vs new values per day) so differences can be shown.
- **FR-011 Screens** (Filament admin, HR + platform admin): **Daily attendance** (date + branch → each office employee's status, arrival, departure, elapsed, presence, lateness, early departure, short hours, balance); **Review queue** (missing punches and absences awaiting HR: add time / confirm absence); **Policy** page (view, add a new version with an effective date); employee day detail showing intervals, exits, the rule values used and the history of recalculations. English first, Arabic RTL; times LTR.
- **FR-012 Permissions, isolation, audit**: HR of the company and platform admins; every table tenant-scoped with composite FKs and isolation tests; HR decisions and policy versions append-only with triggers and audit rows.
- **FR-013 Tests**: PRD §25 test 6 (incomplete ongoing day stays pending), 10 (09:00 → 18:00 not late; 09:15 → 15 min late; leaving 17:50 after 09:15 → 10 min early departure), 11 (single punch → missing punch pending review; correction HR-only), 12 (lateness and early departure separate in detail, combined without double counting); 08:30 → required 17:30; 07:30 → required 17:00; exits reduce presence, not balance; short hours < 8 h alert; rest days (Fri/Sat default, per-employee override, back-dated rest-day change recalculates only affected days); not before hire date / after last working day; pattern missing; device outage grouped issue instead of mass absence; recalculation keeps previous results; policy version recorded; isolation.

## Out of scope

Shift and security employees, shift inference and cross-midnight shifts (later spec); leave coverage and allocation (504); overtime and rest-day work (later); holidays (later — until then a public holiday on a working day appears as `absent` pending review, which HR can confirm or wait for the holidays spec); profile and dashboard (505); notifications.
